Local Resources

Where to Get Care in Cincinnati and Ohio

Ready for the next step? These campus, city, county, and state programs can help you get the HPV vaccine, screening, and follow-up care. Many options are low-cost or no-cost.

On campus

UC University Health Services

What it offers

Urgent care (care for problems that need attention soon but are not emergencies) and primary care (everyday care, like checkups) for UC students, including immunizations (vaccines) and women’s health services (University of Cincinnati, n.d.).

Who it’s for

UC students.

How to get started

Students can ask about the HPV vaccine and cervical cancer screening (University of Cincinnati, n.d.).

Visit Website

Statewide

Ohio Department of Health Breast and Cervical Cancer Project (BCCP)

What it offers

No-cost cervical cancer screening and diagnostic testing (tests that find the cause of an abnormal result) for people who qualify, plus a Patient Navigation Program that helps people find providers, schedule appointments, and understand their insurance (Ohio Department of Health, n.d.).

Who it’s for

People who qualify. A quick phone call can tell you if you do.

How to get started

Call 1-844-430-BCCP (2227) to see if you qualify (Ohio Department of Health, n.d.).

Visit Website

City

Cincinnati Health Department

What it offers

A federally qualified health center (a community health center that receives federal funding) with six primary care health centers offering medical and ob/gyn care (care for reproductive health and pregnancy), each with an on-site pharmacy (City of Cincinnati, n.d.).

Who it’s for

People in Cincinnati looking for primary care or ob/gyn care.

How to get started

Call Community Health Centers at 513-357-7320 (City of Cincinnati, n.d.).

Visit Website

County

Hamilton County Public Health

What it offers

The county health department, offering clinical services, health promotion and education, and vaccine record requests (Hamilton County Public Health, n.d.).

Who it’s for

People who live in Hamilton County.

How to get started

Visit the website to explore services or request your vaccine record.

Not sure where to start?

UC students can start at University Health Services. Not a UC student, or no insurance? Call BCCP at 1-844-430-2227.

Quick Takeaway

You don’t have to figure this out alone. UC students can get care at University Health Services (University of Cincinnati, n.d.), and the BCCP offers no-cost cervical cancer screening to people who qualify (Ohio Department of Health, n.d.).
